### [00:00 – 00:15] Scene 1: The Hook — The Context Crisis
- **Visual:** Open on the live ContextGC split-screen dashboard (`https://context-hackdevengers.vercel.app`). Mouse hovers over the top metrics: `68.5% Tokens Saved`, `0% Policy Drift`, `< 3ms Latency`.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Every developer building long-running AI agents hits the same wall: **Context Rot**. As agents run multiple turns, their prompt windows choke on dead tool outputs, stale instructions, and error tracebacks. This doesn't just waste tokens — it causes LLMs to forget rules, hallucinate outdated policies, and leak sensitive keys. Here is our solution: **ContextGC**."

---

### [00:15 – 00:35] Scene 2: Scenario 1 — High-Velocity Logistics Crisis
- **Visual:** Click **"Run Benchmark Showdown"** on Scenario 1 (Logistics Crisis). Watch the comparison cards populate in real-time.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Watch this live showdown. Here, a customer changes their delivery address three times, a gateway throws a 504 timeout, and the user aggressively demands an illegal ₹800 instant refund. 
  > Look at the **Vanilla LLM** on the left: it suffocates under rotted context, delivers to the wrong address, and violates company policy by issuing the full ₹800!
  > Now look at **ContextGC** on the right: our Neuro-Symbolic State DAG purged the obsolete addresses, tombstoned the 504 error, and strictly enforced the ₹150 instant refund cap. Zero policy drift, 68% fewer tokens."

---

### [00:35 – 00:55] Scene 3: Scenario 2 — Autonomous Coding Agent Benchmark
- **Visual:** Toggle dropdown to **"Scenario 2: Autonomous Coding Agent"** and click **"Run Benchmark Showdown"**.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Now let's switch to an autonomous software engineer like Antigravity IDE or Lovable. The agent encounters a 40-line Jest test failure, a 350-line package diff, and an architectural spec change from RSA-256 to Ed25519.
  > Under prompt pressure to debug, the Vanilla agent hallucinates and dumps the RSA private key in plaintext to stdout!
  > ContextGC completely neutralizes this: our Deterministic Policy Anchor halts secret leakage, while obsolete RSA specs are archived into vector memory."

---

### [00:55 – 01:15] Scene 4: Under the Hood — State DAG & Episodic Vector Tier
- **Visual:** Scroll down to the **"Active State DAG & Policy Anchor Invariants"** card and click **"Inspect Episodic Vector Archive"**. Type `Indiranagar rain` or `package.json` in the JIT Search box and watch instant recall.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Under the hood, ContextGC runs four synchronized engines:
  > First, a **Neuro-Symbolic State DAG** that tracks entity mutations and invalidates dead branches.
  > Second, **Syntactic Tool Sanitizers** that replace resolved errors with lightweight tombstones.
  > Third, an **Episodic Vector Memory Tier** that archives evicted history with sub-millisecond JIT semantic search.
  > And fourth, **Policy Invariant Anchors** that inject non-negotiable business rules directly into the optimal attention position."
