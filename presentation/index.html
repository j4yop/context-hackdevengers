The Architecture

ContextGC: The Neuro-Symbolic Defragmenter

Just like modern operating systems garbage-collect unused memory, ContextGC continuously purges dead conversational branches, distills tool outputs, and anchors invariants.

Core Engine 1 & 2
⚡ State DAG & Invalidation Engine

Maintains a causal dependency graph of active entity mutations. When Turn 4 supersedes Turn 0, Turn 0's dead tokens are atomically evicted.

🛡️ Deterministic Policy Anchoring

Injects immutable business rules (financial refund caps, private key masking) directly at the optimal attention position to halt policy hallucination.

Core Engine 3 & 4

Condenses 350-line package diffs and multi-page error stack traces into compact semantic tombstones once resolved by subsequent turns.

Evicted context isn't lost — it is asynchronously committed to high-speed vector storage with sub-millisecond Just-In-Time (JIT) recall.
